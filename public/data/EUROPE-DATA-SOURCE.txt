Europe country boundaries and capital cities
============================================

Contents
--------
`europe-data.js` defines `window.EUROPE_ATLAS_DATA` with 50 sovereign European
countries (geographic Europe, including transcontinental states commonly
treated as European) and one capital city per country, projected to 975 x 610
SVG units:

  - states[]: { id, name, path, centroid: [x, y], isState: true }
  - cities[]: { id, name, coordinates: [x, y], province }  (`province` matches
    the corresponding state's `name`)

Notes on coverage and editing
-----------------------------
- 50 countries: the 44 UN/widely recognized sovereign states of geographic
  Europe plus Russia, Turkey, and the three South Caucasus states
  (Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan), plus Kosovo and Cyprus.
  Cyprus is rendered as the whole island: the Republic of Cyprus
  (ADM0_A3=CYP), Northern Cyprus (CYN) and the UN Buffer Zone (CNM) from
  Natural Earth are merged into a single "Cyprus" entry.
- Non-sovereign territories are excluded (e.g. Faroe Islands, Isle of Man,
  Guernsey, Jersey, Aland, Gibraltar).
- Clipped to a window of lon [-25, 68], lat [34, 72] (covers Iceland to the
  Urals, Malta/Crete/Cyprus to northern North Cape). Consequently:
    * Russia is shown to ~68 degE (European Russia); the Asian part is clipped.
    * Spain's Canary Islands, Portugal's Azores/Madeira, France's overseas
      departments, the Netherlands/Norway's remote territories (incl. Svalbard)
      are outside the window and are not drawn.
    * Denmark excludes Greenland and the Faroe Islands (separate NE features).
- Ring winding was normalized for spherical (d3-geo) rendering and rings were
  simplified with Douglas-Peucker (tolerance 0.01 deg). Feature bounds for
  projection fitting were computed on the unsimplified geometry.
- Projection: d3.geoConicEqualArea (conical equal-area), parallels 42 and 64,
  centered on [12, 54], fitted to 975 x 610 with 10 px padding.
- Capital coordinates are approximate city-center longitude/latitude in
  WGS84, converted with the same projection. Capital dots may sit a fraction
  of a pixel off coastlines (e.g. Copenhagen, Nicosia) due to generalized
  coastlines; this is invisible at this scale.
- Centroids are planar centroids of the projected country paths; where that
  falls outside the country (e.g. Croatia, Greece), the capital's projected
  coordinates are used instead.
- Monaco, San Marino, Vatican City and Liechtenstein are far below the
  polygon resolution of 1:10m at this scale; their boundaries are indicative
  only.

ID scheme: states `eu-<iso3 lower-case>`, cities `eu-city-<iso3 lower-case>`
(e.g. `eu-fra`, `eu-city-fra`).

This file is generated data; edit the generator inputs, not the file.

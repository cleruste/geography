Africa country boundaries and capital cities
===========================================

Contents
--------
`africa-data.js` defines `window.AFRICA_ATLAS_DATA` with 54 UN member states of
Africa (all playable, `isState: true`), 3 disputed / non-UN-member territories
rendered as non-playable background context (`isState: false`), and 54 capital
cities (one per UN member state), projected to 975 x 610 SVG units:

  - states[]: { id, name, path, centroid: [x, y], isState: true | false }
  - cities[]: { id, name, coordinates: [x, y], province } (`province` matches
    the corresponding playable state's `name`)

Projection and geometry notes
-----------------------------
- Projection: `d3.geoConicEqualArea` centered on [17 degE, 2 degN] with
  standard parallels -18 and 22, fitted to 975 x 610 with 12 px padding.
- Ring winding normalized for spherical rendering; rings simplified with
  Douglas-Peucker (`@turf/turf` `simplify`, tolerance 0.03 deg for mainland/large
  islands, 0.005 deg for small island countries).
- South Africa excludes its remote sub-Antarctic Prince Edward / Marion Islands
  (~46.9 degS) so the continental and African island-nation map fills the
  975 x 610 viewport.
- Small island countries (Cabo Verde, Comoros, Mauritius, São Tomé and Príncipe,
  Seychelles) are gently scaled around their SVG bounding-box centers to a
  minimum span of 12-18 SVG units so they remain visible and clickable at full
  map scale without altering their geographic locations.

Conventions: multi-capital countries and disputed areas
-------------------------------------------------------
- Exactly one representative capital per UN member state is included:
    * South Africa: Pretoria (administrative capital; legislative Cape Town and
      judicial Bloemfontein omitted to keep 1:1 country-to-capital parity).
    * Eswatini: Mbabane (administrative capital; royal/legislative Lobamba omitted).
    * Benin: Porto-Novo (official capital; de facto seat Cotonou omitted).
    * Côte d'Ivoire: Yamoussoukro (official political capital; Abidjan omitted).
    * Tanzania: Dodoma (official capital; Dar es Salaam omitted).
    * Burundi: Gitega (official political capital; Bujumbura omitted).
    * Equatorial Guinea: Ciudad de la Paz (official capital since Jan 2026;
      former capital Malabo omitted).
- Brazzaville (Republic of the Congo) and Kinshasa (Democratic Republic of the
  Congo) sit across the Congo River less than 1 SVG unit apart; their actual
  projected coordinates are preserved.
- Disputed / non-UN-member areas (`isState: false`, excluded from quiz turns):
    * Western Sahara (`af-eh`)
    * Somaliland (`af-somaliland`)
    * Bir Tawil (`af-bir-tawil`)

ID scheme:
  - States: `af-<iso3 lower-case>` (e.g. `af-nga`, `af-ssd`; disputed: `af-eh`,
    `af-somaliland`, `af-bir-tawil`).
  - Cities: `af-city-<iso3 lower-case>` (e.g. `af-city-nga`).
